01 / EcoPeeler Technology

Water-based chemistry engineered for precise wet-process control.

EcoPeeler is a family of aqueous chemicals for semiconductor wet processes, including polymer-residue removal, selective etching and device cleaning. The platform combines reaction control with protection of exposed materials for process-specific performance.

Advanced CORE TechnologyFive technologies designed as one integrated platform

Etching Control

Control of TiN, SiO₂, SiN, Si and Poly-Si etching.

Surface Reaction

Oxide removal, dissolved-oxygen reaction control, CuOx reaction control and galvanic-corrosion control.

Metal Inhibition

Protection of Al, Cu, Co, Ti/TiN, W/WSi and silicide materials.

Surface Protection

Inhibition strategies for Si and SiN surfaces.

Wet Properties

pH control, low viscosity, non-flammability and an aqueous formulation.
